Entangled Vaults uses these third-party assets. Both licenses require attribution,
which is given here, on the title screen and on the pitch screen.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Elementals: Fire Knight (free version) | chierit | CC BY 4.0 | https://chierit.itch.io/elementals-fire-knight | Fireboy's sprite and animations |
| Elementals: Water Priestess (free version) | chierit | CC BY 4.0 | https://chierit.itch.io/elementals-water-priestess | Watergirl's sprite and animations |
| DawnLike 16x16 Universal Rogue-like tileset v1.81 | DragonDePlatino, palette by DawnBringer | CC BY 4.0 | https://opengameart.org/content/dawnlike-16x16-universal-rogue-like-tileset-v181 | Temple bricks, vines, candles, swirl door plaques, the SDS 8x8 pixel font |

chierit asks that the assets not be resold. DawnLike's author asks that games using it
hide the "Platino" sprite somewhere; he is in the Twin Vaults level.

The files in `web/public/assets/` are crops of these packs made by `tools/build_assets.py`.
Everything else (liquids, gems, lens dials, doors, gates, levers, particles, sounds) is drawn
or synthesized in code.
